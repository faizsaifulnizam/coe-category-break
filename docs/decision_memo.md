# Decision memo — levels rose; a window-dependent gap

**2026-10-04 · descriptive, not causal · reviewed for publication**

The long structural read shows a wider median B−A gap: **S$6,190.50 → S$18,198.50 (+12,008)**. It compares 2018-01–2022-04 (98 exercises) with 2022-05–2026-09 R2 (106). Median A rises **36,619 → 96,103** and B **40,005 → 115,051.50**. Gap ranges **−1,198–30,590** and **−2,009–50,335** overlap; the post series is not uniformly higher.

The symmetric calendar read tells a smaller story. In 2021-05–2022-04 versus 2022-05–2023-04, **24 exercises each**, the median gap is **21,745.50 → 24,081.50 (+2,336)**. Median A is **53,209 → 86,000** and B **78,650.50 → 108,028.50**; gap ranges **8,211–30,590** versus **15,355–31,104**. The gap was already large before the rule took effect. Both premium levels rose substantially, without a comparably large tight-window gap shift.

R1-only preserves positive gap-median changes (**+12,398 structural; +2,792.50 tight**). Excluding Apr R1/R2 and May R1/R2 produces **+11,892.50 / +2,832.50**. This is consistency of direction under these specified reads, not universal robustness or a causal estimate. Medians of paired differences cannot be reconstructed by subtracting category medians.

## What this file cannot say

LTA's May-2022 first exercise raised the fully electric Cat A threshold from 97 to **110kW inclusive**, changing basket composition. A/B are competing, connected categories, not treatment and untreated control. Quota allocation, supply-cycle timing, dealer demand, model/buyer mix, EV incentives, macro conditions and March-announcement anticipation can differ across time/categories. The gap can change mechanically through reclassification; this file cannot separate that from behavior or identify how much the policy caused.

**Decision:** communicate the two windows together; retain the categorical refusal of causality. Do not use the long-window +12,008 as an EV-policy effect, or the small tight-window change as proof of no effect. Individual bids, vehicle power/model, registrations, linked substitution and a credible assignment/counterfactual design would strengthen the study. The [annex](if_i_ran_the_test.md) explains why individual buyer randomization would not recover a shared-market equilibrium effect.
